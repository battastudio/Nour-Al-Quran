## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet spiritual reverence, timeless craft, and focused contemplation. Rooted in traditional Islamic manuscript arts and modern digital ergonomics, it treats the screen as illuminated parchment. 

The aesthetic blends classical Andalusian-Ottoman illumination with contemporary editorial precision. The visual demeanor is calm, luxurious, and authoritative—deliberately avoiding trivial gamification, aggressive badges, or noisy micro-interactions. Physical manuscript conventions (gilded dividers, subtle arabesque borders, mihrab arch silhouettes, and authentic Uthmanic Ayah terminals `۝`) are reinterpreted into lightweight, vector-crisp digital elements that honor Quranic sanctity.

Key principles:
- **Right-to-Left (RTL) First**: Natural Arabic optical flow governs all layouts, gestures, and informational pacing.
- **Visual Silence**: Generous margins, breathability around sacred passages, and deliberate contrast to minimize eye strain during long recitation sessions.
- **Offline Reliability**: Clear, tactile visual cues communicate local storage, audio downloads, and offline readiness without cluttering the devotional canvas.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces strict bifurcation between **Sacred Quranic Script** and **Operational UI Elements**.

### Sacred Quranic Text
- **Typeface**: `Amiri Quran` (fallback `Amiri`, followed by traditional Naskh fallbacks).
- **Properties**: Extended line-heights (between `1.9x` and `2.2x` font size) to give diacritics (Harakat, Sukun, Shaddah, and Madd marks) ample breathing space without vertical collisions.
- **Kerning & Ligatures**: `font-feature-settings: "kern" 1, "liga" 1, "dlig" 1` enabled across all Quranic renderings.
- **Ayah End Symbol**: Gilded end-of-verse markers `۝` (U+06DD) encapsulate the verse number rendered with eastern Arabic numerals (`١، ٢، ٣`).

### Interface & Translation Text
- **Typeface**: `IBM Plex Sans Arabic` (fallback `Noto Sans Arabic`).
- **Characteristics**: Clean, neutral, structured, humanist Arabic sans with balanced vertical ascenders and descenders that pairs seamlessly with technical metadata (Juz, Hizb, Page, Audio Playback Timestamps).
- **Translations/Tafsir**: Displayed in `body-md` using a slightly muted ink shade to distinguish editorial reflection from the primary sacred text.

## Layout & Spacing

The reading experience centers on a focused single-column spine (`max-content-width: 780px`) mimicking an authentic Mushaf page layout while gracefully reflowing on responsive viewports.

### Breakpoints & Responsive Behavior
- **Mobile (`< 640px`)**: Single-column edge-to-edge reading with `16px` side margins. Navigation rests anchored in an ergonomic bottom dock. Top bar remains transparent until scroll threshold is reached.
- **Tablet (`640px - 1024px`)**: Constrained `680px` centered Mushaf card frame with subtle Islamic border ornamentation flanking the page boundaries.
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`)**: Dual-pane mode optionally split between sacred text (right pane) and Tafsir/Word-by-Word grammatical analysis (left pane), anchored within a max container of `1200px`.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical cadence follows an `8px` baseline grid. Verses are spaced with `space-lg` to prevent visual blending, while Surah header banners utilize `space-2xl` top and bottom margins to signify clear structural divisions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses subtle parchment layering, gold-leaf borders, and diffused ambient depth rather than harsh synthetic shadows.

- **Level 0 (Canvas)**: Theme-specific `Surface-0`. Flat, non-reflective base.
- **Level 1 (Ayah/Surah Cards & Mushaf Frame)**: `Surface-1` elevated with a 1px inner border (`rgba(184, 144, 46, 0.15)` in light mode, `rgba(212, 178, 90, 0.2)` in dark mode) paired with an ultra-diffuse shadow: `0 8px 24px -6px rgba(15, 26, 20, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Audio Player Bar & Action Drawers)**: Frosted surface with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a directional shadow: `0 -4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Tasmi' Audio Microphone FAB)**: High-prominence jewel component. Elevated with a dual ring: a solid gold edge plus a diffused aura: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(15, 92, 72, 0.35)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Tafsir Sheets)**: Inset scrim `rgba(10, 15, 12, 0.65)` layered beneath a floating panel with `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.28)`.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture is balanced and architectural:
- Standard cards, buttons, and bottom sheets use level 2 roundedness (`0.5rem` to `1rem`), providing modern softness without losing structural dignity.
- Decorative frames integrate classical **Mihrab arches** (ogee arches with continuous curves) for Surah headers, Daily Ayah hero banners, and audio player covers.
- Surah number badges and bookmark icons leverage the **Rub el Hizb (۞)** octagonal 8-point geometric star shape.
- Floating action buttons and audio controls adopt pure circles (`rounded-full`) to signify fluid, continuous audio recitation.

## Components

### 1. Surah Header Banner (ترويسة السورة)
- **Geometry**: Mihrab-inspired arched header card framed in double hairline strokes with gold corners.
- **Content**: Surah name in ornate calligraphy (`surah-title`), flanked by revelation place (مكية / مدنية) and ayah count.
- **Center Ornament**: Classic Basmala (`بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ`) centered with `space-md` separation before Ayah 1.

### 2. Ayah Card & Recitation Item
- **Layout**: Full-width RTL passage block. 
- **Action Strip**: Appears on ayah tap; reveals minimal icons (Play Verse, Tafsir, Copy, Bookmark, Share as Image) aligned horizontally with `space-sm` gaps.
- **Word-by-Word Mode**: Individual words wrap smoothly; tapping a word reveals its transliteration and grammatical root (I'rab) in a micro-popover.

### 3. Tasmi' (تسميع) Audio Microphone FAB
- **Visuals**: Centered, elevated floating circular button (`60px` diameter) breaking through the bottom navigation dock.
- **State**: Default displays a clean gold/white microphone glyph on an accent green surface. When voice recognition or live listening is active, the border pulses with an animated dual-ring golden wave (`pulse-subtle`).

### 4. Bottom Dock Navigation
- **Form**: Floating glassmorphic dock (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), with a curved recess framing the center Tasmi' FAB.
- **Items**: Mushaf (المصحف), Audio Reciters (القراء), Bookmarks (المحفوظات), Search & Tafsir (البحث). Icons are accompanied by concise `label-sm` typography.

### 5. Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Buttons**: Deep accent green fill, gold label text, `0.75rem` border radius, subtle inward gold border.
- **Secondary / Ghost Buttons**: Transparent base with `1px` tinted border matching ink tone at 20% opacity.
- **Sliders & Seekers**: Audio scrubbing track is `4px` slim line with a circular gold handle (`14px`) and buffer progress indicator for offline status.

### 6. Chips & Filters (Juz / Hizb / Rub)
- **Geometry**: Compact capsules (`rounded-full`), `32px` height, featuring eastern Arabic numerals with gentle gold border transitions when active.
- **Behavior**: Horizontal scrolling carousel with edge feathering (CSS `mask-image` linear gradient).
